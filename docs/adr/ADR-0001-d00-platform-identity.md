## Context

The project needs one platform identity. Three names meet here: the new platform, the pilot destination, and the legacy app. Mixing them up breaks links and store listings.

## Options considered

The register records no alternatives. The planning handoff locks this identity for this planning pass.

## Decision

The new platform is guia.la. Cotijuba is the first destination config. `cotiju-app` is legacy only. Historical URLs are not renamed.

## Evidence

Source: [decision register, D-00](../../planning/decisions.md). The handoff states: "Brand and naming (locked for this planning pass)".

## Consequences

Renaming is cheap now. It turns costly after a store listing, so names must be final before the first store release.
