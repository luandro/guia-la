# 14. No account required to read

- **Decision register:** D-13a
- **Status:** approved — master plan §01 "What we intentionally avoid: Accounts required to read public destination information"
- **Date:** 2026-11-21
- **Deciders:** owner (locked in the master plan)
- **Phase gate:** 07
- **Reversal cost:** not stated in the register
- **Related:** [decision register](../../planning/decisions.md); D-13b (no visitor accounts); D-09 (editor authentication)

## Context

Public destination information must be readable without an account. The master plan lists accounts required to read public destination information under what we intentionally avoid.

## Options considered

The register records no alternatives.

## Decision

No account is required to read public destination information.

## Evidence

Source: [decision register, D-13a](../../planning/decisions.md), citing master plan §01.

## Consequences

Public reads stay open. Only editors authenticate (see D-09).
